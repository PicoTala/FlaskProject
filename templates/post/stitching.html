I picked up cross stitch in late 2018, when my grandmother gifted me all her embroidery supplies. I looked around for something fun to make and found Sirithre's "Stardew Valley Cross Stitch Guide". One green junimo and friends later (seen below), I was hooked.

Now, I tend to lurk in Sirithre's discord, and encourage people to try making biscornu for my own selfish enjoyment. I've also tried my hand at designing a few simple patterns, such as a biscornu modeled after Think Quick sprites and some cat slimes for a friend's indie game, Song of Asirra.
